Questions?

Everything parents ask 🧠

My kid is sick — will they really want to do a learning packet?
We don't do generic worksheets, which is why this works on low-energy days. The packet is built around what they're obsessed with right now, with a character carrying them through every subject. Tired kids who won't touch a worksheet will finish a packet that's secretly about their favorite thing.
Do I download a pre-made packet?
No — and that's the whole point. There are no pre-made packets. Every packet is generated from scratch the moment you ask: your kid's grade level, their current obsession, one invented character woven through every subject. What you print didn't exist 60 seconds earlier.
How is this different from free printables I find online?
Free printables are static, one-grade-fits-all, and cost you an hour of hunting and guessing. Ours is generated fresh for your kid's exact grade level and obsession in about 60 seconds — one packet, no scrolling, no printing half of a 200-page pack.
Can the packet be quiet and calm?
Yes. Every packet includes gentle, low-key activities — reading, drawing, and movement breaks that fit a restful day. It's designed for the couch, not the playground.
What ages does it cover?
Kindergarten through 8th grade. Set each kid's level once, and every packet matches it.
Do I need a card for the free packet?
No. One free packet per month, no card, no surprise bills.
